ShapeJoy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Publisher: KhanDevHub · Contact: imran.apps.dev@gmail.com

In short

ShapeJoy: Shape Puzzle is a single-player game that works offline. We do not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

To let you continue where you left off, ShapeJoy saves the following locally on your device only:

  • your settings (sound effects on/off, haptic feedback on/off);
  • your progress (which levels you completed and your best star rating for each);
  • your in-progress puzzle (piece positions, move and hint counts) for each level;
  • two one-time tips that record whether you have already seen them.

This information is not sent anywhere and is not linked to your identity. ShapeJoy has no accounts and no sign-in.

What we do not collect

ShapeJoy does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising identifier, or device identifiers. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it makes no purchases.

Network access and permissions

ShapeJoy is designed to work without an internet connection and does not send information over the network. The app asks for the Android "vibrate" permission only to provide haptic feedback and the audio-settings permission only to play its sound effects. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location or storage.

Third parties

ShapeJoy contains no third-party advertising or analytics services. Downloading and updating the app is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, which have their own privacy policies; we do not receive personal information from them through the app.

Children

ShapeJoy contains no ads, purchases, social features, chat or links, has no accounts, and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete on our side.

Deleting your data

All ShapeJoy data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes it permanently. We hold no copy and have nothing to delete on a server.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this page and the effective date, and update the store privacy disclosures before releasing a version that changes them.
